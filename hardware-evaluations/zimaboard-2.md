# ZimaBoard 2 Evaluation

**Status:** Evaluated — Not Purchased

## Why It Was Considered

ZimaBoard 2 was evaluated as a possible future low-power x86 homelab node. Its combination of an Intel N150 platform, dual 2.5GbE, dual powered SATA, and PCIe expansion makes it relevant to future NAS, networking, container, and lightweight virtualization workloads.

## Potential Roles

- Low-power always-on server
- Small NAS
- Network gateway/firewall
- Container host
- Lightweight virtualization node
- Media/self-hosting node
- Smart-home infrastructure

## Relevant Hardware

The current manufacturer specifications list an Intel N150 processor, 8 GB or 16 GB LPDDR5 memory, 32 GB or 64 GB eMMC, dual 2.5GbE, dual powered SATA 3.0, USB 3.1, PCIe 3.0 expansion, and a fanless enclosure.

Specifications and pricing should be re-verified before purchase because hardware offerings and prices can change.

## Fit With This Homelab

The board is interesting because it combines x86 compatibility, fast networking, storage connectivity, and expansion in a compact form factor. It could complement Raspberry Pi appliances rather than replacing them.

## Decision

**Not purchased.** Revisit if a concrete workload requires the board's particular combination of compact x86 compute, dual 2.5GbE, powered SATA, or PCIe expansion.

This project is an evaluation, not a recommendation to purchase the product.
